**Short answer:** choose a website developer in Rohini who can show live sites they built, gives you a written page and feature list, puts the domain, hosting and code in your name, builds for speed on mobile, sets up SEO and tracking as standard, and is reachable after launch. Use the ten checks below in your first meeting.

Rohini has many website designers — from students and freelancers to agencies around the District Centre. Most of them can make a site look good. The differences show up later: when the site is slow, when it does not rank, or when you need a change and nobody answers.

## The 10-point checklist

### 1. Can they show live websites they built?
Ask for links, not screenshots. Open them on your phone. Check if they load quickly and if the contact buttons work.

### 2. Will you own the domain, hosting and code?
This is the most important question. The domain and hosting should be registered in your name, with logins shared with you. Many businesses have lost their site — and their Google rankings — because the developer owned the account.

### 3. Is the quote written and itemised?
You should get a page list, feature list, timeline and price in writing. "Website — ₹X" is not a quote.

### 4. How fast will the site be on mobile?
Ask what Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) they aim for and whether they test with PageSpeed Insights. Rohini customers mostly browse on phones and mobile data.

### 5. Is SEO setup included?
At minimum: unique titles and descriptions, proper headings, schema markup, a sitemap, Google Search Console and a link to your Google Business Profile. For local businesses, mentioning your sector and area in the content matters.

### 6. Will they set up conversion tracking?
You should be able to see how many calls, WhatsApp clicks and form fills the site produces. Without tracking you cannot tell if the site works.

### 7. Can you edit content yourself?
Ask which CMS they use and whether they will train you. You should be able to change text, photos and timings without paying for every edit.

### 8. Who writes the content?
If you need help with text, check whether copywriting is included. Good content is often what separates a site that brings enquiries from one that does not.

### 9. What happens after launch?
Ask about the free fix period, maintenance plans, response times and how changes are priced. Get it in writing.

### 10. Can they meet you?
A local developer can visit your clinic, shop or office, take photos and understand your customers. It is not essential, but it helps — especially for businesses in Rohini, Pitampura and nearby areas.

## Red flags

- The developer registers the domain in their own name.
- No written scope, only a lump-sum price.
- "Guaranteed first page on Google."
- No examples of live work.
- Payment of 100% upfront for a large project.

## Questions to ask in the first meeting

Copy these into your notes:

1. Can I see three live sites you built in the last year?
2. Whose name will the domain and hosting be in?
3. What is included — pages, content, SEO setup, tracking?
4. What speed do you aim for on mobile?
5. What happens if I need changes after launch?
