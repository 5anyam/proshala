**Short answer:** the best tasks for AI agents are frequent, rule-based jobs that need a little reading or judgement — replying to enquiries, qualifying leads, booking appointments, answering FAQs from your own documents, following up on quotes and payments, and keeping your CRM updated. Start with one, measure it, then add the next.

An **AI agent** is software that reads a message or situation, decides the next step and takes action in your tools — within limits you set. Here are ten tasks where that works well today.

## 1. Replying to new enquiries in seconds

Leads from your website, Google, Meta and IndiaMART get an instant, relevant WhatsApp reply instead of waiting hours for a callback. The agent shares your brochure or price list and asks one or two qualifying questions.

## 2. Qualifying leads

The agent asks what the customer needs, their budget range, location and timeline, then scores the lead and passes hot ones to a salesperson with a summary. Your team stops spending the first ten minutes of every call on basics.

## 3. Booking appointments and calls

Connected to Google Calendar or your booking system, the agent offers available slots, confirms the booking and sends reminders. Clinics, salons, coaching institutes and consultants feel this one immediately.

## 4. Answering FAQs from your own documents

Fees, timings, eligibility, return policies, required documents — an agent answers from **your** approved information and hands over to staff when a question goes beyond it. See [AI chatbots](/services/ai-chatbots).

## 5. Following up on quotes

When a quote has been sent and there is no reply, the agent sends polite reminders on a schedule, answers simple questions and alerts the salesperson if the customer is ready to talk.

## 6. Chasing payments

Overdue invoices get friendly reminders with a UPI payment link. When payment arrives, reminders stop and your records update automatically.

## 7. Updating the CRM after calls

After a sales call, an AI step can summarise the conversation from notes or a recording and update the CRM with next steps — so pipeline data is actually current. See [CRM automation](/services/crm-automation).

## 8. Extracting data from documents

Invoices, purchase orders, KYC forms and bank statements can be read by AI and turned into structured data for your accounts or sheets, with low-confidence items flagged for a person to check.

## 9. Triage of support messages

Incoming emails and WhatsApp messages are sorted by topic and urgency, simple ones are answered, and the rest are routed to the right person with context.

## 10. Daily summaries for the owner

Every evening, an agent compiles leads, bookings, payments and issues from your tools into a short WhatsApp or email summary — no one has to prepare it.

## How to start without risk

1. **Pick one task** with clear success measures.
2. **Write down the rules**: what the agent may and may not do, and when it must hand over.
3. **Test on real past cases** before going live.
4. **Run a supervised pilot** where your team reviews outputs.
5. **Measure and expand.**
