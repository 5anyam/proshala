**Short answer:** in Delhi in 2026, a professionally built small business website typically costs somewhere between **₹25,000 and ₹1,00,000**, an e-commerce store between **₹40,000 and ₹3,00,000+**, and a custom web app from **₹2,00,000 upward**. Template sites from freelancers can cost less than ₹15,000. The range is wide because "a website" can mean very different amounts of work.

## What drives the price of a website?

Four things account for most of the difference between a ₹15,000 quote and a ₹1,50,000 quote.

### 1. Number of unique page designs

A five-page site where every page uses the same layout is quick to build. A site with a distinct homepage, service pages, location pages, a blog and a booking flow needs more design and development time. Count **templates**, not pages: twenty service pages built from one well-designed template cost far less than twenty unique designs.

### 2. Who writes the content

Content is the most underestimated part of a website. If you supply clear, final text and good photos, the build moves fast. If the developer has to interview you, write the copy and source images, that is real work — and it is usually what makes a site actually convert.

### 3. Features and integrations

Each of these adds cost because it adds logic and testing:

- Online booking connected to a calendar
- Payments (Razorpay, UPI, COD rules)
- WhatsApp Business API automation
- CRM, Google Sheets or IndiaMART lead integration
- Member or student logins
- Multiple languages

### 4. Platform

| Platform | Typical use | Upfront cost | Ongoing cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Template / DIY builder | Very small sites | Lowest | Monthly builder fee |
| WordPress | Content-heavy business sites | Low to medium | Hosting + maintenance |
| Shopify | Online stores | Medium | Monthly Shopify plan + apps |
| Next.js | Fast, custom sites and apps | Medium to high | Low hosting, maintenance as needed |

## What hidden costs should you check?

Before you compare quotes, ask each developer about these:

1. **Domain** — renewed yearly; must be registered in *your* name.
2. **Hosting** — monthly or yearly; who pays, and in whose account?
3. **Premium plugins or themes** — some have yearly licences.
4. **Shopify apps** — many charge monthly.
5. **Maintenance** — who updates, backs up and secures the site after launch?
6. **Changes after launch** — how are they priced?
7. **WhatsApp and AI usage** — automation tools have usage-based costs.

## How to compare two website quotes fairly

Ask every company for the same three things in writing: a **page list**, a **features list**, and an **ownership statement** (domain, hosting, code and content belong to you). Then compare like for like. A cheap quote that excludes content, SEO setup and tracking often costs more over a year than a higher quote that includes them.
