**Short answer:** WhatsApp automation uses the official **WhatsApp Business API** to send and answer messages through software. Local businesses use it to reply to every new lead within seconds, send appointment and payment reminders, share order updates and run opt-in offers — with several staff working from one number and every chat connected to their CRM.

## Why WhatsApp, and why automate it?

In Delhi, most customers would rather WhatsApp a business than fill a form or send an email. That is great for conversion, but it creates a problem: messages arrive all day on one phone, replies depend on whoever is free, and nothing is tracked. Automation fixes the speed and the tracking without making conversations robotic.

## WhatsApp Business app vs WhatsApp Business API

| | Business app | Business API |
|---|---|---|
| Users | One phone (plus linked devices) | Many staff via a shared inbox |
| Automation | Greeting, away messages, quick replies | Full automation and chatbots |
| Integrations | None | Website, CRM, Sheets, Shopify, booking tools |
| Bulk messages | Broadcast lists, limited | Approved templates to opted-in customers |
| Cost | Free | Per-conversation charges by Meta + platform fee |

## Five automations that pay off first

### 1. Instant reply to new leads
When someone fills your website form or clicks a click-to-WhatsApp ad, they get a message within seconds with your details and a question to move things forward. Your team gets an alert at the same time.

### 2. Appointment reminders
Confirmations at booking, reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before, with buttons to confirm or reschedule. For clinics and salons, this alone reduces no-shows.

### 3. Payment reminders with UPI links
Invoices and fee reminders go out with a payment link. When the payment is received, reminders stop automatically.

### 4. Order and delivery updates
Online stores send order confirmations, dispatch updates with tracking links, and COD confirmations before shipping — which also reduces fake orders.

### 5. Review requests
After a visit or delivery, a polite message asks satisfied customers for a Google review. More genuine reviews help your Google Business Profile rank in local searches.

## How to stay within Meta's rules

- Message only people who have **opted in**.
- Use **approved templates** for business-initiated messages.
- Keep marketing messages **relevant and infrequent**.
- Make opting out easy.

Following these keeps your number's quality rating high and avoids restrictions.

## What does it cost?
